About Niketani Craft

Niketani Craft was born from the passion of Riya, a dedicated bag enthusiast who has always admired the beauty, craftsmanship, and individuality of thoughtfully designed bags. What began as a personal love for collecting unique bags gradually evolved into a meaningful entrepreneurial journey, driven by a vision to bring the rich artistic heritage of Santiniketan to a wider audience. With this vision in mind, Riya established Niketani Craft as a small business dedicated to offering premium handmade bags that reflect a perfect blend of traditional artistry, timeless elegance, and contemporary style. Each collection is carefully curated, featuring premium-quality leather, exquisite detailing, and exceptional craftsmanship that celebrates the skill and dedication of local artisans. Sourced directly from Santiniketan, our bags carry the essence of Bengal's rich cultural traditions, where every creation tells a story of creativity, patience, and passion. At Niketani Craft, we believe that a bag is more than just an accessory; it is an expression of personality, individuality, and appreciation for authentic craftsmanship. Beyond creating beautiful and durable products, our mission is to support local artisans by providing them with greater visibility and opportunities to showcase their talent, ensuring that their skills, traditions, and artistic legacy continue to thrive in a rapidly changing world. We are committed to building a brand that values quality over quantity, authenticity over mass production, and meaningful craftsmanship over fleeting trends. By connecting customers directly with the artistry of Santiniketan, Niketani Craft hopes to preserve traditional skills while introducing them to modern lifestyles through elegant, practical, and thoughtfully crafted designs. Every bag we offer represents our commitment to quality, our respect for the hands that create it, and our belief that small businesses can make a meaningful difference in the lives of local communities. As we continue to grow, our dream remains simple: to bring the finest of Santiniketan's handmade craftsmanship to every customer while keeping its artistic heritage alive, empowering local artisans, and creating a lasting connection between tradition, creativity, and modern fashion.
